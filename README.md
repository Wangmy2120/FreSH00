## Acknowledgements

We sincerely thank the maintainers and all contributors of the **UEA Multivariate Time Series Classification Archive (2018)** for building and maintaining this invaluable resource.

In particular, we deeply appreciate the tremendous efforts of the data curators and annotators who spent significant time **collecting, cleaning, preprocessing, and standardizing** the datasets used in this project. Their meticulous work in handling missing values, aligning temporal dimensions, and ensuring data quality has laid a solid foundation for reproducible research in multivariate time series analysis, and we are truly grateful for their dedication.

We respectfully cite the original archive as follows:

> Bagnall, A., Dau, H. A., Lines, J., Flynn, M., Large, J., Bostrom, A., Southam, P., & Keogh, E. (2018). The UEA multivariate time series classification archive, 2018. *arXiv preprint arXiv:1811.00075*.

The archive is publicly available at [http://www.timeseriesclassification.com](http://www.timeseriesclassification.com).
